## Puzzle word lists and clue banks

- `assets/puzzles/guess5.txt` is a five-letter word list built from the public-domain
  `web2` word list (Webster's Second International, 1934, as shipped in `/usr/share/dict` on
  macOS and BSD systems), with some modern words and inflections added and offensive words removed.
- `assets/puzzles/crossword.tsv`, `ladder4.txt`, `ladder5.txt`, `weekly_mid.tsv` and
  `weekly_hard.tsv` were written for this project with help from a language model, then
  merged, deduplicated and checked by hand. They are original short definitions, not copied
  from any published puzzle. They are released under the MIT license with the code.
- `assets/puzzles/crowns.jsonl` and `sunmoon.jsonl` are generated by `daybook/puzzles.py`
  with fixed seeds.

The puzzle kinds are public genres (star battle, binary grids, word ladders, mini crosswords,
five-letter word deduction). Their names, boards and answers here are original.
